# Dayan AI — the AI assistant for university stakeholders

**Dayan AI** is the AI assistant for university stakeholders — students, instructors, staff and administrators — built by [Eques AI](https://dayan.equesai.tech/about).

Through one chat, in English or Egyptian Arabic, Dayan AI:

- answers questions about courses, prerequisites and **credit hours**;
- guides departments through **university course scheduling and timetables** phase by phase, over the institution's own data, validating every workbook before the next phase begins;
- produces **schedule records and reports** as documents ready to review and share;
- gives every role exactly what its permissions allow, with the university's own Google sign-in.

[![Dayan AI](https://dayan.equesai.tech/dayan-share-card.png?v=2)](https://dayan.equesai.tech/)

## What makes it different

Dayan AI is not a standalone timetable generator. It is an AI assistant that works over the university's real academic data — courses, curricula, students, instructors and rooms — so every answer, schedule and report reflects the institution as it actually is. See how it compares with traditional timetabling software: [Dayan AI vs traditional scheduling systems](https://dayan.equesai.tech/compare).

## Deployment

- **First deployment:** Heliopolis University (Cairo, Egypt), where members sign in with their `@hu.edu.eg` Google account. Free for Heliopolis University members during the pilot.
- **Access:** limited to the universities Dayan AI is deployed at; sign-in is the university's Google account, role-based (student, instructor, staff, administrator).
- **Languages:** English and Egyptian Arabic (chat and generated documents; Arabic laid out right-to-left).
